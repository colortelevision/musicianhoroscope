Let the imperfections and wow-and-flutter guide your melody rather than correcting every frequency. Keep your studio cozy; a warm ambient pad is your safest harbor from the outside world.

Let a collaborator take the lead solo while you dial in the perfect sidechain compression to make the collective groove pump. Your stage presence is undeniable, even if you're just streaming a bedroom modular set.

Virgo

Libra

You’re seeking perfect phase alignment in your relationships and your mixes today. Balance the high-end sparkle of your ambitions with a solid, grounded sub-bass reality.

Aquarius

Your radical approach to sound design is exactly what your current project needs to break the mold.

Lean into this ambient drift, but set a sleep timer so you don't get completely lost in outer space. A beautiful, melancholic melody is waiting to be caught if you can stop tweaking the delay feedback long enough to hit record.
