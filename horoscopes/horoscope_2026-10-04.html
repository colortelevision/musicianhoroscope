Musician's Horoscope

Seldom Records — October 04, 2026
MOON ALERT: The Moon drifts through an introspective frequency today, inviting warm tape hiss and slower tempos. Avoid making irreversible bounces or impulse bidding on boutique Eurorack modules on Reverb after 8 PM.

Aries

Your natural impulse is to slam the preamps into red, but today’s transit demands subtle gain staging. Channel that fiery drive into dialing in a crunchy analog overdrive pedal rather than arguing with your mix engineer. Give your dynamic transients room to breathe before engaging the bus compressor.

Taurus

You're locked into a hypnotic four-bar loop, and honestly, there is no need to break free just yet. Ground yourself in thick Moog sub-bass and let the gentle drift of an uncalibrated oscillator soothe your stubborn streak. Keep your patch cables organized; studio clutter will cloud your creative frequency.

Gemini

Your brain feels like a stereo delay with independent left-right division rates today. You have six unfinished sessions open, but the stars suggest consolidating down to one stereo track and committing. Turn off the Wi-Fi, unplug the MIDI controller, and play a physical instrument straight to tape.

Cancer

Nostalgia runs deep under this cosmic alignment, pulling you toward dusty field recordings and warbly cassette loops. An old voice memo holds the exact melodic phrase you need to resolve your current project’s bridge. Protect your creative aura the same way you’d protect a vintage ribbon mic from phantom power.

Leo

The spotlight is hitting your master channel, Leo, but check your wet/dry balance before going live. It is tempting to smother your lead lines in lush shimmer reverb, but raw vulnerability will resonate more with your listeners. Let the melody stand naked on a dry track for a few takes.

Virgo

You are micro-nudging MIDI notes by three ticks when what the arrangement really needs is natural, human swing. Step away from the DAW surgical EQ and listen to the room through some warm, unhyped monitors. Perfection is the enemy of the groove; embrace the tape flutter.

Libra

Harmonic convergence is possible today, especially if you’re collaborating across different genres. You excel at balancing the stereo field, but don't compromise on your signature sonic identity just to keep the jam session peaceful. Trust your gut when it comes to leveling the final mix.

Scorpio

Subterranean basslines and detuned minor chords are your sanctuary right now. Dive into deep sound design and don't be afraid to route your signals through unconventional pedals in reverse order. Keep your unreleased demos close to your chest; their power lies in the mystery.

Sagittarius

Your wanderlust is itching for a tour van road trip, even if you’re just carrying a portable sampler to a local coffee shop. Break out of your conventional chord progressions by switching to an alternate tuning or an odd-meter sequencer track. A chance sonic discovery outside your comfort zone will spark your next EP.

Capricorn

Treat your studio time like the sacred ritual it is, but remember that discipline doesn't mean rigidity. Building rigid drum templates can lay a solid foundation, but leave room for an unpredictable modular burst. Long-term royalties reward those who patiently finish the arrangement rather than archiving the loop.

Aquarius

You're tuned into an avant-garde broadcast that no one else can quite hear yet. Route your control voltage into unintended patch points and see what happy accidents arise from the feedback loop. The establishment might not understand your lo-fi micro-tonal experiment, but Seldom-style purists will get it.

Pisces

You are floating in an ocean of 100% wet space echo, completely untethered from the grid. While the ambient textures are sublime, make sure to anchor the track with a solid kick drum before you drift out of phase entirely. Let your emotional intuition guide the filter sweep.

IF YOUR BIRTHDAY IS TODAY: You enter this solar year with the warmth of a fully warmed-up tube amplifier. Like pristine analog hardware, you only grow richer with character, color, and vintage patina over time. May your signal path stay clean, your headroom stay generous, and your master fader bring quiet triumph.
