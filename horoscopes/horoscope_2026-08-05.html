Taurus

Gemini

Cancer

Leo

Virgo

Mercury urges you to clean up your messy project folders, color-code your DAW tracks, and fix those lingering phase alignment issues. Precision is your superpower today, but don't quantize the soul completely out of the performance. A little human swing keeps the groove alive.

Libra

Venus glues your mix bus together, helping you find sweet harmony between heavy low-end and silky high frequencies. It’s a prime day for a chill collaboration, provided you and your co-writer can agree on a BPM. Keep your arrangement spacious and let the reverb tails breathe.

Scorpio

Pluto digs deep into the dark corners of your synth rig, unlocking brooding basslines and industrial textures. Sagittarius

Jupiter expands your sonic horizons, pushing you toward microtonal tuning, experimental pedal chains, or outdoor field recordings. Step away from the studio desk and capture the ambient noise of the city to feed into your sampler. Your best hook today comes from an unexpected frequency.

Capricorn

Aquarius

Uranus sparks a wild breakthrough in your signal flow—expect happy accidents when routing effects in reverse order. An unconventional patching decision will unlock the precise hook you’ve been chasing all week. Break a few golden rules of mix engineering just to see what unfolds.

Pisces

Neptune bathes your creative flow in a dreamlike, side-chained wash of delay trails and tape flutter. You are hyper-tuned to texture and mood today, making it effortless to build deep, transportive soundscapes. Capture your ideas immediately before the dreamy haze clears.
