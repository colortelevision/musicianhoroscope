Taurus

You are feeling stubborn about your sound aesthetic today, refusing to bounce anything that hasn't run through a physical tube preamp. You have too many musical ideas competing for the same track, so try committing to a single tempo. Pick one synth lead and write a solid hook instead of starting six new projects you will abandon by midnight.

Cancer

The cosmic weather is calling for ambient pads and heavy reverb, Cancer. Retreat into your cozy home studio corner, light some incense, and let the field recordings of rain do the emotional heavy lifting. It is a great day for writing melancholic chord progressions, but a terrible day for negotiating split sheets or contract terms.

Leo

The spotlight is hunting you down, Leo, so make sure your stage presence is as polished as your signal path. Whether you are performing live or just posting a synth jam reel, your charisma is running through a high-end booster pedal. Just remember to let your bandmates have some headroom in the final mix, too.

Virgo

Your inner sound engineer is running wild today, Virgo, tempting you to spend six hours micro-editing transient splices that nobody else will ever hear. Step away from the zoom-in tool and let the track breathe. Perfection is the enemy of the groove, and a little bit of human timing error is where the magic lives.

Libra

You are seeking total stereo balance today, Libra, trying to harmonize your high-end aspirations with your low-budget gear reality. It is a fantastic time for collaborative jam sessions, as your diplomatic energy can effortlessly bridge the gap between a stubborn guitarist and a techno purist. Keep your panning wide and your mind open.

Scorpio

Your creative energy is diving straight into the sub-bass frequencies today, Scorpio, searching for raw emotion in the dark and muddy depths. Don't be afraid to write something eerie, experimental, or completely unmarketable. The universe wants you to explore the shadow side of your synthesizer, but do not forget to check your mix on regular speakers eventually.

Capricorn

Your work ethic is unmatched right now, but remember to occasionally unplug and jam just for the sake of the art.

Aquarius

Keep pushing the boundaries of sound design, but make sure your master fader is not clipping into pure digital distortion.

Pisces

Your mind is drifting through a 100% wet delay signal today, Pisces, making it hard to focus on linear tasks like arranging a traditional verse-chorus structure.
