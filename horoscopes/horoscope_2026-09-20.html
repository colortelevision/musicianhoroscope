Gemini

Your brain is cross-modulating at audio rate, leaving you with twenty unfinished project folders and a tangled nest of cables. Pick one solitary tape loop and commit to carving out its frequencies before starting another sketch. Collapse those dual-mono thoughts into a clean, focused stereo image.

Cancer

Dig into your dusty cassette stash or run ambient field recordings through a guitar pedalboard for comforting, lo-fi warmth. Leo

The spotlight naturally finds your pedalboard, but the cosmos warn against overplaying during someone else's delicate verse. Step back and let the bassline breathe, trusting that your presence is felt even during the rests. A tasteful touch of spring reverb will showcase your star power far better than raw decibels.

Virgo

With the Sun illuminating your sign, your internal EQ is operating at surgical, microscopic precision. Be wary of notching out the quirky imperfections that give your track its human pulse; clinical cleanliness is the enemy of groove today. Leave a little analog hiss in the intro.

Libra

The approaching equinox pulls your focus toward stereo symmetry and elegant, balanced arrangements. You may find yourself paralyzed trying to pick between two snare samples, but the stars suggest neither is wrong—it's simply a matter of gain staging. Trust your monitoring environment and close the arrangement window.

Scorpio

Deep, subterranean sub-bass frequencies speak directly to your intuition today. Keep your works-in-progress and unreleased stems closely guarded, as loose talk in the green room could give away your best sonic secrets. Channel your brooding intensity into a hypnotic, minor-key modular drone.

Sagittarius

Your artistic wanderlust is translating into wide-open stereo imaging and cavernous, infinite-decay reverbs. While expansive cinematic sketches come effortlessly, make sure someone checks the phase correlation before your low end disappears in mono. Ground your cosmic exploration with a steady, unquantized kick drum.

Capricorn

Discipline is your favorite instrument, making this the perfect afternoon to label patch bays and archive old session drives. Saturn rewards your relentless work ethic with a sudden breakthrough on an arrangement that has felt creatively stalled for months. Treat your creative habits like high-end studio gear: reliable, direct, and built to last.

Aquarius

You are wired for generative sequencing, strange clock dividers, and routing schemes that break traditional music theory. If your usual DAW feels sterile, abandon the screen entirely and see what happens when you patch audio signals into control voltage inputs. Your weirdest modulation accident is destined to become the main hook.

Pisces

You are drifting happily through a lush sea of modulated chorus, pitch-drift, and ambient tape flutter. While the atmosphere is undeniably dreamy, your mix is at risk of drowning in mud unless you ruthlessly high-pass the low-mids. Pull yourself out of the reverb wash just long enough to print your tracks.

IF YOUR BIRTHDAY IS TODAY: Born on this day, your internal clock is master-synced to analog elegance, sonic depth, and meticulous craft. Trust the warmth in your natural imperfections.
