Seldom Records — October 10, 2026
MOON ALERT: The Moon drifts through late Libra into a hazy analog saturation with Neptune. Print your master bus to cassette before midday; after that, logic gives way to pitch drift and spontaneous modulation.

Aries

Your transient response is hitting the red today, but clipping isn't always the enemy. Channel that aggressive overdrive into a raw drum pattern on your sequencer instead of clashing with your collaborators. Back off the gain staging just enough to let the sub-bass breathe.

Taurus

Venus wraps your signal path in thick, bucket-brigade delay, tempting you to stay looped inside an eight-bar ambient drone forever. Indulge in the tactile weight of wooden synth cheeks and dusty low-pass filters today. Tomorrow you can worry about arrangement structure and commercial viability.

Gemini

Your mind resembles a patchbay wired by an anxious cat, spitting out polyrhythms faster than you can sequence them. Pick one monophonic voice, commit it to audio, and refuse to touch the preset selector again. Friction creates character, while infinite digital options will only breed studio paralysis.

Cancer

Nostalgia is heavily saturating your tape heads today, pulling you back toward childhood chords and bittersweet sampling sessions. Feed an acoustic memory into an SP-404 vinyl simulator and let the wow and flutter heal whatever Mercury rattled earlier this week. Protect your creative sanctuary from harsh digital clocks.

Leo

Even in a dimly lit, cable-strewn basement studio, your lead lines demand center stage and pure tube warmth. Today is ideal for carving out an iconic hook on an analog monosynth with the resonance screaming and the cutoff wide open. Just make sure you leave adequate headroom for the rest of the ensemble.

Virgo

You are tempted to spend four hours micro-tuning sidechain compression thresholds and nudging audio transients three milliseconds to the left. Step away from the surgical EQ and learn to love the hiss; the magic lives inside the timing jitter. Imperfection is the only human thing left in the signal chain.

Libra

Balance isn't a sterile center pan today—it’s the gentle push-pull between a dry signal and lush stereo chorus. A creative collaboration could yield pure gold if you stop over-polishing the core tempo to appease someone else's taste. Trust your inner ear over the visual frequency analyzer on your screen.

Scorpio

Dark, minor-key drone textures are calling your name from the depths of an obscure FM synthesis menu. Dig deep into the shadow frequencies and unearth the low-end weight you’ve been far too polite to mix in. What you synthesize in secret today will define your aesthetic for the coming winter.

Sagittarius

Wanderlust has seized your signal chain; you're ready to pack an OP-1, hit the open road, and capture field recordings of distant train crossings. Avoid locking yourself into rigid MIDI grids right now and track everything completely freehand. The universe wants you to capture acoustic drift, not robotic quantization.

Capricorn

Discipline meets vintage architecture today as Saturn stabilizes your workflow. It’s the perfect cosmic window to finish that sprawling concept album or finally solder those faulty quarter-inch patch cables piling up in the corner. Solid grounding prevents ground-loop hum in both your circuits and your contracts.

Aquarius

An erratic patch cable connection in your modular rack might spark an accidental avant-garde masterpiece this afternoon. Don't immediately troubleshoot the error—record the glitch, pitch it down two octaves, and build an entire groove around the anomaly. Conventional song structures are completely useless to you under this transit.

Pisces

The boundaries between your subconscious dreams and your reverb decay times are virtually nonexistent today. Drench an electric piano in endless tape delay, crank the feedback to ninety-nine percent, and drift through the harmonics. Just remember to hit record before you dissolve entirely into the room's noise floor.

IF YOUR BIRTHDAY IS TODAY: Born today, your creative soul runs at 3.75 IPS—warm, nostalgic, yet rhythmically precise. This upcoming year invites you to step away from clinical digital polish and commit to tactile, hardware-driven art. Trust the gentle tape degradation; your legacy will be measured by emotional resonance, not your loudness war victory.
