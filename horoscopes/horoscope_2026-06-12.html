Libra

Today is all about finding the perfect panning and frequency balance in your life's stereo field. If a collaboration feels out of phase, a gentle EQ cut in the mid-range will restore the harmony. Trust your ears, but don't forget to check your mono compatibility.

Scorpio

You're digging deep into the sub-bass frequencies of your psyche today, Scorpio. Don't fear the dark, distorted textures of a modular feedback loop; that's where your best art lives. Just make sure you put a limiter on the master track so you don't blow your subwoofers.

Sagittarius

Your creative spirit is craving a field recording expedition or an unpredictable random voltage generator. Break out of your usual 4/4 grid and let your sequencer run in an odd time signature. The journey is the destination, even if your VCOs go completely out of tune.

Capricorn

Aquarius

Your mind is patch-wired directly into the year 3000 today, generating avant-garde microtonal melodies. While others might not understand your DAW-less ambient generative patches, stay true to your weird frequencies. The mainstream will catch up to your sidechain compression techniques eventually.

Pisces

You are swimming in a lush, 100% wet reverb wash today, Pisces. While it is easy to get lost in the dreamy atmosphere of space-echo delays, don't forget to anchor yourself with a solid kick drum. Let your intuition guide your patch cords, but watch out for digital clipping.

IF YOUR BIRTHDAY IS TODAY: Happy solar return! This year, your life path is getting some beautiful tape saturation and a fresh set of patch cables. Expect new collaborations that feel like a perfectly synced MIDI clock—harmonic, effortless, and deeply groovy. Keep your tape heads clean.
