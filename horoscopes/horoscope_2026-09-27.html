Musician's Horoscope

Seldom Records — September 27, 2026
MOON ALERT: Cosmic wow-and-flutter is running high today, sending subtle pitch drift into your emotional signal chain. Avoid hard-clipping your relationships; keep a gentle limiter on your reactions and balance your stereo field.

Aries

Mars tempts you to slam the master bus into the red, but raw gain won't fix an arrangement problem. Step away from the aggressive transient shapers and let the rhythm find its groove with some natural swing. Your best take was the spontaneous first run anyway—stop second-guessing the tape.

Taurus

Venus lures you into a four-hour rabbit hole auditioning vintage tape saturators and warm analog preamps. Indulging your sonic palate is healthy, but make sure you actually lay down the core chords before dinner. The lush bottom-end you're seeking is already dialed in; just trust your monitors.

Gemini

Your studio desk is a tangled jungle of half-patched Eurorack cables and open browser tabs of synth forums. Channel that scattered, dual-brain energy into a split-stereo soundscape where two opposing melodic ideas converse. Remember to hit record before that bizarre modulation loop disappears into the ether.

Cancer

Nostalgia hits through a lush bucket-brigade delay, pulling forgotten chord progressions out of your subconscious. Capture that tender, late-September melancholy using field recordings and warm acoustic layers straight into your sampler. Protect your vulnerable dynamic range from unsolicited critiques today.

Leo

The vanity of over-produced vocal stacks is calling, but today's stars favor the honesty of a single dynamic mic in an untreated room. Let the natural room bleed and unvarnished vocal character become your centerpiece. You don't need a wall of synth brass to demand the listener's attention.

Virgo

Mercury has you fixated on notch-filtering a tiny resonance at 3.1 kHz until the life is drained from the track. Zoom out from the microscopic waveform and listen to the whole mix with your eyes closed. Imperfection is where the analog soul lives—leave a little room for tape hiss.

Libra

With the Sun illuminating your sign, your innate sense of stereo balance, harmonic spacing, and aesthetic cohesion is at an all-time peak. You are the master glue compressor of your social circle, effortlessly smoothing out everyone's harsh peaks. Just don't let option paralysis stall your choice of snare tone.

Scorpio

Subterranean bass frequencies and moody minor scales provide the sonic architecture for your day. You're ready to mute half the arrangement to let heavy, brooding ghost notes linger in deep digital reverb. Keep your unreleased stems guarded; not everyone is cleared to hear the rough mix.

Sagittarius

Your creative wanderlust demands portable gear—pack a handheld sampler and take your session to the porch or an empty stairwell. The cosmos is transmitting weird, beautiful micro-samples if you're open to off-grid inspiration. Don't worry about standard time signatures; let the tempo breathe.

Capricorn

Saturn brings methodical discipline, making this the ultimate afternoon to color-code your DAW tracks or solder failing cables. Treat your songwriting workflow like an old Japanese step sequencer: steady, calculated, and hypnotic. The slow groundwork you lay behind the mixing desk today will pay dividends on future masters.

Aquarius

You're drawn to anti-music today, patching an LFO directly into an unintended cv-input just to see what glitches out. Reject cookie-cutter presets and build your own experimental wavetable from scratch. The strange audio artifact everyone else considers an error is actually your next hook.

Pisces

Neptune dissolves the threshold between conscious playing and ambient drift, making it easy to float inside a 100% wet reverb wash for hours. While the dreamlike synth pad is stunning, ground the low-end with a solid kick drum before you drift completely away. Write down the lyric before the tape echo fades to silence.

IF YOUR BIRTHDAY IS TODAY: Happy Solar Return! This year invites you to strip away digital sheen in favor of tactile, organic grit and honest songcraft. Your signature sound will emerge through creative constraints, vintage textures, and unquantized instincts. Keep your faders clean, your head warm, and never sell your favorite polyphonic synth.
