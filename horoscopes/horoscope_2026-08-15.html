Aries

Taurus

Gemini

Your brain is running polyrhythmic arpeggios at breakneck tempo, leaving collaborators struggling to catch the beat. Pick a single four-bar loop and live inside it before introducing ten new modulation sources. Cohesion happens when you commit to one groove.

Cancer

Leo

Virgo

Libra

Scorpio

Sagittarius

Capricorn

Your work ethic is unshakeable, but even the tightest master clock needs a little swing to keep people moving. Loosen your rigid session templates and experiment with free-running LFOs and un-synced delays. Structure should serve the muse, not the project management sheet.

Aquarius

Futuristic sound design and unconventional patch routing are heavily favored in your chart today. You are hearing arrangements from five years in the future, so patch the unpatchable and print the happy accidents. Ground your avant-garde ideas with a solid, repetitive kick drum.

Pisces

You are swimming in lush, endless shimmer reverbs and dreamy bucket-brigade delay tails today. Record your stream-of-consciousness improvisations straight to two-track before the analytical editing mind takes over. The ghost notes you play by accident contain the real magic.
